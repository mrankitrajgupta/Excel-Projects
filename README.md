# 📊 Excel for Business Specialization

Welcome to my **Excel for Business Specialization** repository!

This repository contains Excel assignment projects completed as part of the **Excel for Business Specialization**, along with additional projects independently developed using the knowledge and skills gained throughout the course.

The projects focus on applying Microsoft Excel to practical data-related tasks, including data cleaning, analysis, business insights, reporting, and visualization.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Develop practical Microsoft Excel skills.
- Apply course concepts to real-world datasets and business scenarios.
- Learn data cleaning and preparation techniques.
- Analyze datasets to identify patterns and trends.
- Extract meaningful business insights from data.
- Create reports and visualizations to communicate findings.
- Build interactive dashboards for effective data presentation.

## 🛠️ Skills & Concepts

- Data Cleaning and Preparation
- Data Sorting and Filtering
- Excel Formulas and Functions
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Data Analysis and Summarization
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Dashboards and Slicers
- Business Reporting and Insights

## 💻 Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, analysis, visualization, and dashboard development.

This repository serves as a collection of my Excel learning and practical work. It includes both course-based assignments and independently developed projects that demonstrate my ability to apply Excel concepts to different datasets and business problems.
